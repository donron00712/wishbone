Why it works

Out-of-home is the last channel nobody can close.

Digital advertising competes for a screen its audience is actively trying to get past. Out-of-home works on different terms — it shares physical space with people. Kismat Cookies takes that logic to the smallest placement in the category: the table.

The category

What physical media does for a brand.

Four things out-of-home does that a bought impression cannot.

  • 01

    It cannot be closed, skipped or blocked

    Every digital format competes for a screen its audience is trying to get past. Out-of-home occupies physical space instead, so there is no ad blocker, no skip button and no auction for the attention.

  • 02

    It works on memory rather than clicks

    Out-of-home is bought to shape how a brand is remembered, not to drive an action in the next ten seconds. That is a different job from performance media, and a different thing to measure.

  • 03

    Taking up space is a signal in itself

    Putting something physical into the world is a visible commitment. It is a quality an impression bought at auction does not carry, whatever the targeting.

  • 04

    It addresses a place, not a profile

    Out-of-home speaks to everyone present rather than to a segment. At a dinner table that means a brand can arrive with a group rather than an individual.

The placement

Why the cookie is the sharpest version of it.

A billboard is passed at distance. A fortune is handed across the table.

  • 01

    It arrives as part of the meal

    The cookie comes with the check the way it always has. Your message is inside something the restaurant is already handing over, rather than an interruption bought against someone’s attention.

  • 02

    It lands at the calmest point of the evening

    The end of a meal is unhurried in a way almost no other advertising moment is. Nothing else is bidding for the table at that point.

  • 03

    It reaches a table, not a screen

    A cookie goes to each person present, so a brand can arrive with a group in the same moment rather than one device at a time.

  • 04

    It is a physical thing, not an impression

    The slip is small enough to pocket and leaves the restaurant with whoever takes it. It does not disappear when a feed refreshes.
