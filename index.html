The unit

It arrives with the check, as part of the meal.

An ordinary fortune cookie from the outside. Inside is the slip: your line, your brand and the lucky numbers, printed to your brief.

Illustrative example

Why it works

Out-of-home, at the smallest possible scale.

Advertising that shares physical space with people cannot be blocked, skipped or scrolled past. This is that idea, shrunk to fit in a hand.

  • 01

    It arrives as part of the meal

    The cookie comes with the check the way it always has. Your message is inside something the restaurant is already handing over, rather than an interruption bought against someone’s attention.

  • 02

    It lands at the calmest point of the evening

    The end of a meal is unhurried in a way almost no other advertising moment is. Nothing else is bidding for the table at that point.

  • 03

    It reaches a table, not a screen

    A cookie goes to each person present, so a brand can arrive with a group in the same moment rather than one device at a time.

  • 04

    It is a physical thing, not an impression

    The slip is small enough to pocket and leaves the restaurant with whoever takes it. It does not disappear when a feed refreshes.

What you get

Six ways to put a brand inside a fortune cookie.

Mix them, or start with the fortune and nothing else.

  • The fortune

    Eleven words on the slip

    The line inside the cookie, written with you or by you. Eleven words is the whole creative brief — short enough to take in at a glance, long enough to carry a voice.

    See the formats
  • The numbers

    Lucky numbers that actually do something

    Every fortune carries its six numbers. They can be a discount code, a draw entry or a store number instead of decoration.

    See the formats
  • The code

    A prize on the back of the slip

    A QR or promo code on the reverse, so the fortune has something to redeem and you have something to measure.

    See the formats
  • Targeting

    City, cuisine, daypart

    Choose where the cookies land: a neighbourhood, a cuisine, a dinner rush, a single street. Placement is a media buy, not a mailing list.

    See the formats
  • The drop

    Limited runs for a moment

    Short, dated runs built around a launch, a holiday, a fixture, a Lunar New Year service. Scarcity the table can feel.

    See the formats
  • Co-branding

    Two brands, two sides

    The slip has a front and a back. A line each, or a line and a code — a natural format for a collaboration where both sides get their own surface.
